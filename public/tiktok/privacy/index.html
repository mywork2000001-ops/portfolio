Privacy Policy

What we collect

When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive from TikTok only: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), creator settings needed for posting, and OAuth access/refresh tokens. We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

How we use it

Solely to upload and publish Fizmatix videos to the connected account and to check the publishing status. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on our own server (Oracle Cloud, access restricted by SSH key), never in public code repositories, and are transmitted only to TikTok over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account remains connected. Disconnect the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or e-mail us, and the tokens are deleted within 7 days.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children; viewers of the videos interact only with TikTok, not with this app.
